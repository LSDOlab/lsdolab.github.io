lsdo_geo

Geometry parametrization.

Version 1.0

Source code ↗Documentation ↗

lsdo_geo parameterizes shapes for multidisciplinary design optimization using free-form deformation and B-spline sectional methods, with analytic derivatives through CSDL. It projects aerodynamic and structural meshes onto the geometry, and builds on lsdo_function_spaces. The lab's geometry parameterization and geometric non-interference research extend these capabilities for aircraft and robotic configurations.
