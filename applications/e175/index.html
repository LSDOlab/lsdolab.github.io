Aerospace

E175 aircraft

Aerodynamic shape design

We use an E175-based lift-constrained drag-minimization study as a capability test of the lab's differentiable surface-mesh deformation method. Rather than propose an E175 redesign, the demonstration asks whether full-configuration Euler CFD can remain coupled to a conforming volume mesh as planform, wing position, mounting angle, twist, camber, and tail rotation change.

Demonstrating mesh deformation inside MDO

The Mach-0.82 demonstration uses inviscid OpenFOAM Euler CFD with DAFoam sensitivities, about 100,000 triangular surface elements, and about 1.7 million tetrahedral volume cells. Elasticity-based volume-mesh deformation keeps the CFD mesh coupled as the 42 design variables change. The result is a capability demonstration, not a validated redesign of the E175.

Paper: Ruh and Hwang [1]
