Marius L. Ruh

PhD candidate

Develops differentiable mesh deformation and geometry tools for large aircraft shape changes, with work on electric air taxis and blended-wing-body aircraft.

Education

  • PhD, Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering
    UC San Diego · 2022–present
  • MS, Mechanical Engineering
    UC San Diego · 2022
  • BS, Aerospace Engineering
    UC San Diego · 2020
