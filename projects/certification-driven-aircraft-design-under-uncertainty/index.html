Certification Driven Aircraft Design Under Uncertainty

NASA · University Leadership Initiative

Collaborating institutions: Virginia Tech (lead), Auburn University, Stevens Institute of Technology, Tidal Flight, UC San Diego, Tuskegee University, Aurora Flight Sciences, The Boeing Company

This project integrates model-based systems engineering, multidisciplinary design, analysis and optimization, and high-dimensional uncertainty quantification to enable faster, more efficient modeling for next-generation aircraft design, with fewer costly redesigns later in development.
