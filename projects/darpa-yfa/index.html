This project pioneers large-scale multidisciplinary design optimization as a sizing tool for power-beaming-enabled UAVs, replacing lost design-database knowledge with physics-based models of structures, thermal behavior, aerodynamics, motors, batteries and photovoltaic cells. It addresses coupling to the energy web, expensive trajectory analysis, and large technology, mission and model uncertainties.
